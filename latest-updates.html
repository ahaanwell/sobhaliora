Sobha Liora Latest Updates

Sobha Liora Latest Updates indicate that this upcoming premium residential project is moving toward its pre-launch phase in Whitefield, one of East Bangalore's fastest-growing residential corridors. Located at Immadihalli, the development introduces luxury apartments with spacious configurations and modern planning. For homebuyers and investors tracking Sobha Liora and the evolving Sobha Whitefield portfolio, the project has started attracting attention because of its location, planned infrastructure, and limited inventory.

The latest available project information highlights a thoughtfully planned residential community spread across 7 acres, featuring four residential towers and premium 3, 3.5, and 4 BHK apartments.

Sobha Liora Latest Updates: Current Project Status

The latest updates suggest that Sobha Liora is currently in the pre-launch stage, with the project preparing for its official market launch. Pricing details are yet to be announced, while the RERA registration remains under the approval process.

Key project highlights currently available include:

  • Premium luxury apartment development
  • 7-acre land parcel
  • 420 planned apartments
  • Four residential towers
  • Structure: 2 Basements + Ground + 17 Floors
  • Possession timeline: 31 December 2030
  • Developed by Sobha Limited

As additional approvals and launch activities progress, more information regarding apartment sizes, pricing, floor plans, and booking schedules is expected to become available.

Sobha Whitefield Project Receives Attention for Strategic Location

One of the biggest highlights in the Sobha Liora Latest Updates is its location in Immadihalli, Whitefield, a well-established residential neighbourhood in East Bangalore.

Excellent Metro Connectivity

The project enjoys access to the Kadugodi (Whitefield) Metro Station, improving daily connectivity across Bangalore. Metro accessibility continues to play an important role in reducing travel time for professionals working in Whitefield and surrounding employment hubs.

The location also benefits from established social infrastructure, including schools, healthcare facilities, shopping destinations, and commercial developments that have gradually transformed Whitefield into a mature residential market.

Premium Apartment Configurations Planned

  • 4 BHK Apartments
