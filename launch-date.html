Sobha Liora Launch Date

The Sobha Liora Launch Date is one of the most searched topics among homebuyers looking for premium residential projects in East Bangalore. Located at Immadihalli, Whitefield, Sobha Liora is currently in the pre-launch stage and is expected to be officially launched between mid-August 2026 and early September 2026, subject to regulatory approvals and the developer's official announcement.

Developed by Sobha Limited, Sobha Liora is planned as a premium apartment community spread across 7 acres with 420 luxury apartments. This article provides the latest available information on the project's launch timeline, current status, and what buyers can expect during the launch phase.

Sobha Liora Launch Date: Latest Project Update

Based on the latest project information, the Sobha Liora Launch Date is expected between mid-August 2026 and early September 2026. The project is presently in its pre-launch phase, with preparations underway before the official launch.

As of now:

  • Project Status: Pre-Launch
  • Expected Launch: Mid-August 2026 to Early September 2026
  • RERA Status: Approval Awaited
  • Starting Price: ₹ On Request
  • Possession Timeline: 31 December 2030

Since the project is awaiting RERA approval, the official launch schedule may be confirmed once all regulatory formalities are completed.

Sobha Whitefield Location Advantage

One of the important aspects supporting Sobha Whitefield developments is the location itself.

Sobha Liora is situated in Immadihalli, Whitefield, one of East Bangalore's well-developed residential and employment corridors. The area offers access to IT parks, commercial offices, educational institutions, healthcare facilities, shopping centres, and everyday conveniences.

An additional connectivity advantage is the project's proximity to the Kadugodi (Whitefield) Metro Station, which provides improved public transportation options for residents travelling across Bangalore.

The combination of established infrastructure and metro connectivity continues to support residential demand in this part of the city.

Final Thoughts on Sobha Liora Launch Date

The Sobha Liora Launch Date is currently expected to fall between mid-August 2026 and early September 2026, making it one of the upcoming premium residential launches in Whitefield, East Bangalore.
